### 📊 Data Analyst Projects — BI & Dashboards

#### 🏠 [Real Estate Market Intelligence Dashboard](https://github.com/rohandhobale07/Real-Estate-Market-Intelligence-Dashboard)
**Problem:** 100,000 housing records across 607 cities are useless without a way to actually query market trends.
**Solution:** Migrated records into a validated MySQL backend, engineered 10 DAX measures (YoY growth, price-per-SQM, 12-month trends).
**Impact:** Delivered a 3-page, 24-visual Power BI dashboard for interactive market analysis by city and house type.

#### 🛒 [Retail Sales & Returns Performance Analysis](https://github.com/rohandhobale07/Retail-Sales-Returns-Performance-Analysis-)
**Problem:** 51,290 orders across 147 countries were hiding a return-rate problem no one had isolated.
**Solution:** Blended 3 relational data sources into one Tableau model, built 9 interactive visualizations.
**Impact:** Surfaced a **4.7% return rate** and pinpointed the specific underperforming regions and reps driving it.

#### 💳 [Credit Risk Segmentation Dashboard](https://github.com/rohandhobale07/Loan_Default_Analysis)
**Problem:** 255,347 loan records needed to be turned into an actionable risk-assessment tool for a lending team.
**Solution:** Engineered 17 DAX measures to segment loans by credit score, income, age, and employment type.
**Impact:** Identified an **11.6% overall default rate** and built the 3-page, 16-visual dashboard used to act on it.

### 🤖 Data Science Projects — ML & Prediction

#### ✈️ [Flight Fare Prediction](https://github.com/rohandhobale07/Flight_Price_Predictions)
**Problem:** Flight prices swing on date, route, and airline in ways that aren't obvious from raw booking data.
**Solution:** Engineered 12 features from 10,682 records, benchmarked 6 regression algorithms (AdaBoost, Extra Trees, Random Forest, CatBoost, LightGBM, XGBoost).
**Impact:** CatBoost hit **84.15% R²** on unseen data — deployed as a live app, not left in a notebook.

#### 🧑‍💼 [HR Attrition Prediction](https://github.com/rohandhobale07/HR_Department)
**Problem:** A company loses employees and doesn't know who's actually at risk until they've already left.
**Solution:** Diagnosed a 16.1% attrition rate across 1,470 employees, then fixed a class-imbalance problem masking at-risk cases.
**Impact:** Raised recall on departing employees **from 40% to 70%** — a deliberate trade-off of 12 accuracy points for materially better early detection.

#### 🎯 [Customer Segmentation for Targeted Marketing](https://github.com/rohandhobale07/Marketting_Department)
**Problem:** Treating every customer the same wastes marketing spend on the wrong message.
**Solution:** Clustered customer behavioral data using K-Means (validated via Elbow Method + silhouette score), reduced to 2D via PCA for interpretability.
**Impact:** Delivered **6 actionable, reproducible segments** ready to hand to a marketing team.
